Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to common questions.

What are your support hours?

Our customer service line is open 24 hours a day, 7 days a week. You can reach a live representative at (888) 663-0257 at any time.

How do I reset my password?

Call the support line and a representative will verify your identity and walk you through a secure reset.

How long do transfers take?

Timing depends on the transfer type and the institutions involved. Call us for a status update on a specific transfer.

How do I report fraud?

Call (888) 663-0257 immediately. Our fraud team is available around the clock and can freeze activity on your account.

Can I get help outside the United States?

Yes. International clients can call the same number; standard international rates may apply.
